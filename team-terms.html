MYMERCHNOW · Teams

Team service and delivery terms

Effective October 22, 2026. This is a separate MYMERCHNOW service.

Monthly Team link

The organizer explicitly starts a $20 USD monthly subscription after setup approval. It renews until canceled. No setup submission automatically charges a card. Cancel or schedule an off-season pause through the private organizer dashboard. Access remains through the paid month, then new orders stop; paid garment orders still receive fulfillment. Restart requires a new subscription checkout.

Earn a free month

Ten qualifying paid garments within one subscription billing period earns one $20 credit on the following Team renewal invoice. It does not discount garment purchases or shipping. There is no stacking, rollover or cash payout. Orders count after Stripe confirms payment. Canceled, refunded or disputed units are excluded. Partial monetary refunds conservatively reduce the eligible count by whole garment units; contact MYMERCHNOW if the refund concerned shipping only. Eligibility is finalized when the renewal invoice is prepared. An already applied credit is not charged back automatically.

Individual and combined shipping

Individual shipping follows MYMERCHNOW’s quantity rates: $10 for 1–9 garments, $15 for 10–19, and $20 for 20 or more, currently within the United States. A combined team batch requires one address and MYMERCHNOW capacity approval. The organizer pays $25 once for the approved shipment; members are not each charged this fee. The organizer receives and distributes that shipment.

Ordering opens after the combined shipping payment is confirmed. The organizer chooses a window of 7–14 days. The displayed cutoff closes new batch orders automatically; paid orders move to MYMERCHNOW review even if the expected total was not reached. Payments completed after the cutoff require review and are not silently added to a closed batch. The ordering window is separate from production and delivery timing. Review current shipping information before purchase.

Artwork and production

MYMERCHNOW controls garment prices, available products and print methods. Logos stay within approved garment areas. Previews are visual references; original artwork and placement instructions accompany paid production orders. You must have permission to use submitted artwork. Members need no customer login. Design packages are stored privately for order fulfillment. Abandoned, unpaid packages are eligible for deletion after fourteen days; keep your own downloaded design copy. Paid production files remain available for fulfillment.

Help and refunds

Subscription and shipping refund requests are reviewed individually. Apparel orders follow the applicable MYMERCHNOW returns policy. Contact MYMERCHNOW support for changes, expired private organizer access or order questions.
