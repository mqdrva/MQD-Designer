A few useful details

What happens after I request a pilot?

Complete your shop setup and confirm your business email. We review the setup before activating your branded link. Your seven free days begin at approval, so setup time doesn’t use your pilot.

How do customer payments work?

Customers send unpaid design and quote requests. Your shop checks artwork, garment availability and final pricing, then arranges payment and production directly. Estimates exclude tax and shipping.

What happens after the seven days?

The pilot does not turn into a paid subscription automatically. Separately choose the $49/month 2D plan or $99/month 2D + 3D plan when you’re ready. Expired or paused links stop accepting requests.

3. Printing methods and prices

Select only the methods your business offers. Standard Transfer, DTF, DTG and HTV use automatic estimates from your rates. Screen Printing and Sublimation require a shop quote.

Each print rate is per garment. Front and back are charged separately when used; the sleeve rate covers one or both sleeves. A setup fee applies once per request.

4. All-over printing in 3D

These prices include the garment and all-over print. Choose garments and available sizes. Customers choose artwork and colors in the 3D designer; its garment zones define the all-over design. No rectangular print dimensions are needed here.
