A few things to know

Which range should I choose for chemical exposure?

Choose the Premium Performance shirt range if chemical resistance is part of your workwear needs. Everyday cotton apparel is not chemical resistant. Review the individual garment details before ordering.

Can I put the same business logo on both ranges?

Yes. Premium lets you build an all-over layout; Everyday places your artwork within selected print areas. MQD can help you choose a layout for the garment you want.

What does the example price include?

These are short-sleeve shirt examples, not an order quote. The Everyday example includes a standard-transfer front print in S–XL. Your selected garment, size, print method, extra print areas, and current offers determine the designer price. Shipping is calculated in the cart.

When will my order be ready?

Turnaround is usually 2–3 weeks after payment; higher order volume may require an additional week. Review our shipping and turnaround details and custom-order return policy before ordering.
