Seven-day unpaid pilot

Initial availability is limited to five reviewed businesses. Your pilot begins after your email is confirmed and MYMERCHNOW approves your setup. No card or subscription payment is collected. The pilot does not automatically convert to a paid subscription. After approval, choose a monthly plan from your private owner page when you are ready.

How your shop link works

  1. Share your branded shop link with your customers.
  2. Customers create their design and can save a design ZIP to their device.
  3. They send an order request with their sizes, quantities and required contact details.
  4. Your business receives the package by email, confirms the final quote and collects payment directly.

The demo uses sample prices. Business setup requires email confirmation and review. Your unpaid pilot never becomes a paid subscription automatically. The print shop approves artwork and production requirements. Request email packages are limited to 8 MB.
