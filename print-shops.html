How the pilot works

  1. Open the sample shop link and create a design.
  2. Save a design ZIP to your device, then test reopening it.
  3. Submit an order request with your sizes and contact details.
  4. The MYMERCHNOW pilot owner receives the package by email and finishes the sale directly.

This is a test service. Sample shop prices are estimates. Business setup submission and review are available. Subscription payments remain disabled during the pilot. The print shop approves artwork and production requirements. Request email packages are limited to 8 MB.
