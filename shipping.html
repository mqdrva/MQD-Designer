Shipping

Every MQD product is custom prepared from the design submitted with your order.

Last updated September 16, 2026

Shipping rates

Standard United States shipping is calculated from the total number of items in the cart:

Total itemsStandard shipping
1–9 items$10
10–19 items$15
20 or more items$20

The exact shipping charge is displayed before you continue to secure checkout and again in Stripe Checkout.

Processing and turnaround

Once paid, turnaround is usually 2–3 weeks. If we are experiencing a higher volume of orders, we ask for an additional one-week grace period in case we need extra time for production or order handling.

Production begins after payment is confirmed. Because each item is custom, preparation time can vary with the product, quantity, and design. We will contact you if an order needs additional information or if its expected timing changes.

Shipping address

Customers enter and confirm the shipping address during secure checkout. Contact us immediately if an address must be corrected. We may be unable to change an address after production or shipment begins.

Tracking and delivery issues

Tracking information will be provided when available. If a package is delayed, missing, or arrives damaged, email mqdrva@gmail.com with your order reference.
