Use the hood as part of the design

The hood can carry brand color, a pattern, or supporting artwork that ties the top of the garment into the rest of the layout instead of looking like an afterthought.

Keep the back readable

If your company name, phone number, or service wording is important, make sure the hood does not visually compete with the main message on the back. Use the 3D preview to check the garment from behind.

Coordinate the sleeves

Matching or complementary sleeve graphics can make the shirt feel like a complete uniform rather than a front logo placed on a blank garment.
