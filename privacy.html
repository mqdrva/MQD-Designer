Customer policy

Privacy

This policy explains the information Morales Quality Designs uses to provide the designer, customer accounts, and custom-order services.

Last updated September 16, 2026

Information we collect

  • Account information, such as your email address and sign-in identifier.
  • Design content, uploaded artwork, saved designs, mockups, and production files.
  • Order details, including products, sizes, quantities, prices, payment status, contact details, and shipping address.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

How we use information

We use information to save and reopen designs, create and fulfill orders, provide customer support, prevent misuse, secure accounts, and maintain the service.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate the website. Supabase supports account, database, and file-storage features; Stripe processes checkout and payment information; Google may provide optional account sign-in; and Vercel hosts the website. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices. Morales Quality Designs does not store complete payment-card numbers.

Device storage

The designer may use browser storage to keep cart, design, and checkout-recovery information on your device. Clearing browser data may remove information that has not yet synchronized to your account.

Retention and sharing

We keep information as reasonably needed to provide orders, maintain business and tax records, resolve disputes, and protect the service. We do not sell customer personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect customers, the business, or the service.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by emailing mqdrva@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security, or order-fulfillment reasons.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version and its update date will appear on this page.
