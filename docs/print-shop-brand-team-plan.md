# Separate Print Shop, Brand and Team services

Print shops and brands that produce their own garments retain the $49 2D / $99 2D+3D subscription and unpaid email-request flow. Team members order from MYMERCHNOW through a separate merchant checkout using MYMERCHNOW-controlled prices. Main apparel checkout, cart and order tables remain unchanged.

Teams: $20 monthly after explicit organizer checkout. Thirty qualifying net-paid garments during a subscription month earns one $20 credit on the next Team renewal invoice. No stacking, rollover or cash conversion. Refunds and disputes reduce eligibility. Credits are scoped to the owned Team invoice and are idempotent.

Delivery: members may buy individual shipping at existing $10/$15/$20 quantity rates. An organizer can request one combined shipment with a 7–14 day ordering window and expected quantity. MYMERCHNOW reviews address/capacity for the single $25 shipping fee. Ordering opens after confirmed organizer payment. Cutoff closure is automatic; paid batches move to owner review even below their expected quantity. Late payments need review. A funded second batch waits until the current batch closes. Paid orders continue to fulfillment when the software subscription pauses or is terminated.

Each garment can have customer artwork only, an optional movable original logo, or an immutable fixed logo. Fixed logos are reinserted from approved settings on load/export and checked by the backend. Polo positions support center or left chest; existing print-shop defaults remain left chest. Production ZIPs contain originals, logo placement, and rendered Team production zones. Orders receive a server-verified manifest. Previews remain visual references requiring production review.

Private organizer access uses the verified setup-email link and per-tab token storage. Operator access requires fresh administrator role and MFA. Team accounts, batches, orders, periods, rewards and action records use separate private RLS tables. Test/live records are isolated. Production files live in a private Team bucket with admin-only short-lived downloads. Abandoned unpaid files are eligible for cleanup after 14 days, with Stripe status checked before removal. Cutoff and maintenance jobs do not touch regular apparel tables.

Implementation is staged. Team payment collection is disabled. The $20 sandbox price, dedicated portal and signed webhook endpoint are configured; a separately scoped sandbox restricted key needs explicit creation/save approval before real sandbox checkout tests. Live configuration and release remain pending. No real customer has been approved, billed or emailed by these tests. Existing Print Shop direct Stripe management controls also remain gated until their separate management key is configured.
