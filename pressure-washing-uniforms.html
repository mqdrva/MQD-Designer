Use the same core logo, colors, type style, and supporting graphics across multiple garments. MyMerchNow lets you design each garment's available print zones separately while keeping the visual direction consistent.

Bleach and chemical resistant apparel

MyMerchNow Premium Performance products are bleach resistant and chemical resistant. That makes the catalog especially relevant for pressure washing and soft washing businesses that want branded apparel intended for environments where cleaning chemicals may be present.

Match the garment to the job

Use short sleeves for warm-weather crews, long sleeves or hooded tops for additional coverage, polos for estimates and customer-facing work, lightweight jackets for cooler conditions, and hats as a simple accessory that keeps the company identity visible.
