Why resistance matters

Pressure washing and soft washing crews work in environments where apparel can be exposed to cleaning chemicals. MyMerchNow garments are bleach resistant and chemical resistant, while the design workflow lets you keep company branding visible across the front, back, sleeves, collar, or hood depending on the garment.

Build the shirt around your brand

Upload your company logo, remove the background when needed, set the garment colors, add service wording or contact details, and place each element in the print areas that make sense for your crew. The interactive 3D preview helps you check the full garment before checkout.

Use more than one garment

A pressure washing company may need short sleeve shirts for hot days, long sleeves or hooded shirts for sun coverage, polos for estimates and sales calls, and jackets for cooler weather. Because the same visual direction can be applied across multiple products, your team can stay recognizable without every piece looking unrelated.
