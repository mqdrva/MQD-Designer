Questions

Custom Long Sleeve T-Shirt FAQ

Can MQD design the shirts for me?

Yes. Send a free design request with your garment, quantity, and ideas. MQD will confirm options and pricing before creating your mockup. No order or payment is placed by submitting the request.

When does production begin?

Production begins after payment is confirmed. See our shipping and turnaround information and contact MQD before ordering if you have a specific deadline.

Can I design more than the front?

Yes. This product supports Front, Back, Left Sleeve, Right Sleeve, Collar. Each area can be designed separately in the editor.

Can I see the design before ordering?

Yes. MyMerchNow shows the garment in both a flat design workspace and an interactive 3D preview.

Can I save the design and come back later?

Yes. You can save designs to an account, and guest purchasing is also available.
