Questions

Custom Pressure Washing Shirts FAQ

Can MQD design the shirts for me?

Yes. Send a free design request with your garment, quantity, and ideas. MQD will confirm options and pricing before creating your mockup. No order or payment is placed by submitting the request.

When does production begin?

Production begins after payment is confirmed. See our shipping and turnaround information and contact MQD before ordering if you have a specific deadline.

Are MyMerchNow pressure washing shirts bleach and chemical resistant?

Yes. All MyMerchNow products are bleach resistant and chemical resistant.

Can I use my existing business logo?

Yes. Upload your own artwork and position it in the available print zones. The editor also includes background-removal controls for compatible uploaded images.

Can the back be different from the front?

Yes. On garments with separate front and back zones, you can build each side independently.

Can I create matching apparel for different seasons?

Yes. MyMerchNow includes multiple shirts, polos, hooded options, jackets, bottoms, hats, and a mask so you can adapt a consistent brand direction across garments.
