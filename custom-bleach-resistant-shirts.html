Who uses bleach resistant work shirts?

Pressure washing, soft washing, cleaning, exterior maintenance, and other service businesses may work around bleach or cleaning chemicals. Branded workwear can help keep the company easy to identify while the crew is on a property, in customer photos, or traveling between jobs.
